What this is

Crypto Signals is an informational and educational tool. It shows prices, moving averages and technical indicators (RSI, Stochastic RSI and MFI) for the largest cryptocurrencies, and labels coins as Good, Great or Incredible Buy or Sell using fixed indicator thresholds.

Not advice

Nothing on this page is investment, financial, tax or legal advice, or a recommendation, offer or solicitation to buy, sell or hold any asset. The signals are mechanical outputs of a formula. They do not know your goals, finances or risk tolerance. Consider speaking to a licensed financial adviser before making decisions.

Risks

Cryptocurrencies are highly volatile and you can lose some or all of the money you put in. Technical indicators are based on past prices and do not predict the future. A coin can look oversold or overbought for a long time, and signals can be wrong.

Data accuracy

Prices and rankings come from CoinGecko and price history from Binance. Data may be delayed, incomplete or wrong, may differ from other sources, and is saved in your browser for a couple of hours. Coins not listed on Binance have no indicators. Website links are provided for convenience and may be outdated.
