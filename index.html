About

How the price levels work

Each coin gets a price level from Very Low to Very High, based on three momentum indicators on daily candles. RSI is 14-day with Wilder smoothing. Stoch RSI is a 14-day stochastic of the RSI, smoothed over 3 days. MFI is 14-day, using typical price (high + low + close, divided by 3) times volume. The latest reading includes today’s candle, which is still forming, so values shift during the day. The levels describe indicator readings, not the price figure alone.

  • All three indicators must qualify at the same time. If one is outside its range the level is Normal.
  • Levels are checked in this order: Very Low, Low, Low (looser), Very High, High, High (looser). The first match wins.
  • Market regime: bull when Bitcoin trades above its 200-day average, otherwise bear. The looser Low thresholds only apply in a bull market and the looser High thresholds only in a bear market. If Bitcoin data fails to load, neither looser threshold applies.
  • Stablecoins (a fixed list, plus any coin whose 5-year range stays between $0.50 and $1.50) show “Stable” with no level. Coins not listed on Binance, or with too little history, show n/a.

Crypto Signals is an informational and educational tool. It shows prices, moving averages and technical indicators (RSI, Stochastic RSI and MFI) for the largest cryptocurrencies, and labels coins from Very Low to Very High using fixed indicator thresholds.

Risks

Cryptocurrencies are highly volatile and you can lose some or all of the money you put in. Technical indicators are based on past prices and do not predict the future. A coin can look oversold or overbought for a long time, and the levels can be wrong.

Data accuracy

Prices and rankings come from CoinMarketCap and price history from Binance. Data may be delayed, incomplete or wrong, may differ from other sources, and is saved in your browser for a couple of hours. Coins not listed on Binance have no indicators.
